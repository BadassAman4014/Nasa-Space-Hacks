# Field Analytics Data Audit

**Audit date:** 15 November 2026  
**Application:** FieldShift AI  
**Field tested:** Field 2, Cottbus Farm, 12.34 ha  
**Method:** Playwright browser exploration, API inspection, source-code review, and read-only dataset queries.

## 1. Executive summary

Field Analytics now has four visible tabs:

- **Field Map**: field boundary, satellite basemap, moisture/nitrogen/leaching overlays and a compact field card.
- **Field Health**: recommended actions, vegetation trend, soil moisture trend, yield zones, erosion, leaching and nitrogen inputs.
- **Status**: current weather, planting record, Sentinel-2 scenes, NDVI map, NDVI history, precipitation and GDD.
- **Data**: NASA Challenge dataset catalogue and per-field query controls.

The strongest operational experience is Field Map plus Field Health. These combine actual Sentinel-2, SoilGrids, SMAP L4, GPM IMERG, Open-Meteo, DWD and DEM data into field-level decisions.

The Status tab also uses real Sentinel-2 data and NASA POWER values, but its precipitation and GDD period is fixed to **1 March 2024–31 October 2024** for the tested field. The Data tab requires caution: it lists 23 datasets, but many query responses contain hardcoded demonstration values or regional fallbacks rather than a live field-specific observation.

The most important audit conclusion is:

> A successful HTTP response does not mean that the displayed dataset value is live. The Data tab must distinguish catalogue metadata, live measurements, derived values, model output and demonstration placeholders.

## 3. Field Map

### 3.1 Field boundary

**Displayed value**

- Field polygon for Field 2.
- Area: 12.34 ha.
- Farm: Cottbus Farm.
- Current crop: no crop assigned.

**Source**

- Saved field record from `user_farms_repository`.
- Boundary originally imported from DFBK, the Digitales Feldblockkataster for Brandenburg.
- Boundary geometry is served through the saved-field API and rendered with Leaflet.

**Purpose**

- Defines the spatial area for all field-level calculations.
- Clips Sentinel-2 imagery to the field.
- Sets the map extent and provides the clickable field object.

**Interpretation**

The polygon is the field boundary used by the application. It is not an estimate generated by the current Field Analytics page.

**Agricultural use**

- Check whether analysis covers the intended parcel.
- Compare the boundary with the satellite image.
- Identify adjacent parcels that should not be included in decisions.

### 3.2 Field colour: soil moisture status

**Displayed value**

Field 2 displays:

- **Low moisture**
- **Drier than usual**

**Source**

- NASA SMAP L4, `SPL4SMGP.008`.
- Root-zone soil moisture, 0–100 cm.
- Approximately 9 km cell.
- Current value for the tested field: approximately 0.074 m³/m³.
- The value is ranked against about 310 same-season comparison days from 2016–2025 within ±15 days of the current date.

**Current interpretation**

The current root-zone value is below the historical comparison range for this time of year. The application classifies the field red when the value is below the 20th percentile, yellow between the 20th and 40th percentile, and green at or above the 40th percentile.

**Agricultural use**

- Decide whether to avoid sowing into a dry seedbed.
- Prioritise water-conserving tillage.
- Compare moisture status between fields.
- Treat the value as a regional field-area signal, not a measurement at every point in the field.

**Important limitation**

A 9 km SMAP cell is larger than a 12.34 ha field. SMAP cannot show moisture differences inside the field. The in-field map uses Sentinel-2 NDMI or ECOSTRESS when available.

### 3.3 Last rain

**Displayed value**

- 19 days since a rainfall day with at least 1 mm.
- 1.1 mm in the last 7 days.

**Source**

- NASA GPM IMERG Late Run, `GPM_3IMERGDL.07`.
- 0.1° grid, approximately 10–11 km.
- Satellite estimate, not gauge-corrected.
- Tested data was available through 13 November 2026.

**Purpose**

Provides recent rainfall context for moisture stress, soil workability and leaching risk.

**Agricultural use**

- Combine with SMAP to distinguish a recent dry period from a longer-term dry condition.
- Assess whether nitrogen could move below the root zone.
- Support decisions about field access and sowing conditions.

### 3.4 Soil health score

**Displayed value**

Field 2 displays:

- 97 / 100.
- Label: Good.
- Weakest component: compaction / bulk density.

**Source**

- ISRIC SoilGrids v2.0.
- 0–30 cm topsoil profile.
- Approximately 250 m resolution.

**Components**

| Component | Meaning | Field 2 value | Role in score |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Soil reaction | pH and nutrient availability context | pH 6.2 | Full score inside the configured target band |
| Organic carbon | Soil organic matter proxy and aggregation support | 2.66 % | Full score above the configured target |
| CEC | Nutrient-holding capacity | 237 mmol(c)/kg | Full score above the configured target |
| Bulk density | Compaction / pore-space indicator | 1.44 g/cm³ | 88.6 / 100 |

**Purpose**

Condenses several soil properties into an easy screening indicator.

**Interpretation**

This is a FieldShift-derived indicator, not an official soil health index. It should be used to identify which soil property needs attention, not as a regulatory classification.

**Agricultural use**

- Prioritise soil sampling.
- Identify compaction checks.
- Compare fields using the same transparent scoring rule.
- Support cover-crop and organic-matter decisions.

## 4. Field Map overlays

Only one overlay should be active at a time to keep the map readable.

### 4.1 Moisture overlay

**Primary source**

- ECOSTRESS Evaporative Stress Index at 70 m when a recent usable overpass is available.
- Sentinel-2 NDMI at approximately 20 m as the fallback.

**What it represents**

- ECOSTRESS ESI: the relationship between actual and potential evapotranspiration.
- Sentinel-2 NDMI: plant water content / vegetation moisture signal.

**What it does not represent**

It is not direct in-field soil moisture. SMAP provides the broader soil-moisture condition; Sentinel-2 and ECOSTRESS provide finer spatial patterns of plant or evaporative water stress.

**Agricultural use**

- Identify zones for scouting.
- Prioritise drought-stress checks.
- Compare suspected dry areas with field position, soil texture and yield zones.
- Support irrigation decisions only when combined with ground checks and local agronomy.

### 4.2 Nitrogen overlay

**Source**

- Sentinel-2 red-edge bands.
- NDRE is calculated relative to the field.

**Displayed meaning**

- Red: lowest third of the field's relative red-edge response.
- Yellow: middle third.
- Green: highest third.

**Purpose**

Shows relative crop nitrogen uptake or chlorophyll differences inside a growing crop.

**Agricultural use**

- Identify zones for scouting.
- Compare weak areas with soil texture and moisture.
- Support variable-rate investigation.

**Limitations**

- It is not a fertiliser recommendation.
- It is not a legal N calculation.
- It is not used when mean NDVI indicates a bare or sparse crop.
- Low NDRE can result from water stress, crop stage, disease or other causes.

### 4.3 Leaching overlay

**Source**

- SoilGrids sand and CEC.
- GPM IMERG rainfall.
- Open-Meteo forecast.
- Farmer-entered fertiliser date and amount when available.

**Spatial behaviour**

The current application shows leaching risk for the whole field rather than pretending to map precise subfield leaching zones. SoilGrids is too coarse to support detailed within-field nitrogen-risk pixels for these fields.

## 5. Field Health page

### 5.1 Recommended actions

The page turns several datasets into short decision cards.

Examples observed for Field 2:

- Soil drier than usual.
- DWD frost warning.
- Moderate wind-erosion risk.
- Fertiliser blocking period.

Each card has an information button containing the evidence used to create it.

**Purpose**

The farmer sees a decision first rather than a list of measurements.

**Agricultural use**

- Prioritise today's scouting and field work.
- Avoid applying nitrogen before heavy rain.
- Protect soil during bare or windy periods.
- React to official warnings.

### 5.2 Vegetation trend, three years

**Displayed value**

- Field-average NDVI line chart.
- Dates of available Sentinel-2 observations.
- One peak value per season for the April–July comparison window.
- Trend status: improving, stable or declining.

**Source**

- Copernicus Sentinel-2 L2A.
- B04 red and B08 near-infrared reflectance.
- SCL cloud and invalid-pixel mask.
- Real scenes from the Element84 Earth Search STAC catalogue.
- Field 2 audit: 57 scenes from January 2023 through November 2026.

**Method**

- Each scene produces a field-mean NDVI.
- The highest April–July value is selected for each season.
- A linear trend is calculated across seasonal peaks.
- Absolute slope at or below 0.02 NDVI/year is classified as stable.

**Interpretation**

A stable result means spring vegetation response has not changed materially across the available seasons. It does not mean yield is stable, because crop type, planting date and weather also influence NDVI.

**Agricultural use**

- Identify fields that need investigation.
- Compare crop performance across seasons.
- Look for persistent decline before visible yield loss.
- Combine with soil and moisture data to form a hypothesis.

### 5.3 Soil moisture trend, 30 days

**Displayed value**

- Purple line: SMAP L4 root-zone model, 0–100 cm.
- Green band: usual range for the same seasonal period from historical SMAP L4 values.
- Measured surface dots appear when quality-controlled SMAP L3 values are available.

**Source**

- SMAP L4 cloud OPeNDAP, direct 9 km cell reads.
- Historical comparison: same cell, ±15 days around the current date, 2016–2025.
- SMAP L3 Enhanced for quality-filtered surface retrievals.

**Agricultural use**

- Distinguish current dry conditions from normal seasonal variation.
- Assess whether a field is entering moisture stress.
- Combine the trend with rainfall and crop stage.

**Limitations**

- Both SMAP products are coarse compared with a farm field.
- L4 is modelled, not a direct probe measurement.
- L3 measurements can be missing or rejected by quality flags.

### 5.4 Yield zones

**Displayed value**

- Satellite-derived High, Medium and Low relative productivity zones.
- Area and percentage of the field for each zone.
- A map overlay.
- Number of seasons used.

**Source**

- Sentinel-2 NDVI grids stored in the Field Analytics database.
- Existing `productivity_service.py`.

**Method**

- Uses real NDVI grids with adequate valid-pixel coverage.
- Builds a per-season relative rank.
- Combines seasons and divides the field into three relative zones.
- Requires at least two qualifying seasons in the current implementation.

**Interpretation**

This is relative productivity potential, not measured harvested yield. A high zone has stronger repeated NDVI performance than other parts of this field.

**Agricultural use**

- Prioritise soil sampling.
- Compare persistent weak areas with slope, moisture and soil properties.
- Support future variable-rate trials after ground validation.

### 5.5 Erosion risk

**Inputs**

- Copernicus DEM elevation and slope.
- SoilGrids sand, silt, organic carbon and bulk-density information.
- GPM IMERG heavy-rain days.
- Sentinel-2 bare-soil share.

**Output**

- Green, yellow or red screening light.
- Dominant risk: wind or water.
- One practical recommendation.

**Agricultural use**

- Keep sandy soil covered.
- Leave residue after harvest.
- Work across slopes.
- Consider grass strips on lower field edges.

**Limitation**

The current calculation is a FieldShift screening rule, not a formal DIN 19708 erosion assessment.

### 5.6 Leaching risk

**Inputs**

- Soil texture and CEC from SoilGrids.
- Recent IMERG rainfall.
- Open-Meteo rainfall forecast.
- Farmer-entered nitrogen application date and amount.
- DüV blocking-period rule.

**Output**

- Low, moderate or high risk.
- Alerts after or before heavy rain.
- Cover-crop seeding-window status.

**Agricultural use**

- Hold nitrogen before heavy rain.
- Avoid applications during blocking periods.
- Protect sandy soils with cover crops.

### 5.7 Nitrogen estimate

**Inputs entered by the farmer**

- Crop.
- Yield target.
- Previous crop.
- Spring Nmin.
- Organic N applied last year.
- N applied this season.
- Last application date.

**Output**

- Estimated kg N/ha requirement.
- Amount remaining after recorded application.
- Calculation breakdown.

**Important distinction**

This is an estimate following the structure of the German DüV calculation. It is not a legal N ceiling and does not replace the official Düngebedarfsermittlung or BESyD calculation.

### Data tab implementation risks

The following code patterns are present in `backend/app/services/nasa_challenge/client.py`:

- Hardcoded SMAP, GPM, SRTM, ECOSTRESS and Sentinel-2 values.
- Fallback profiles after failed external requests.
- Synthetic CMR metadata when the CMR request fails.
- US-only fallback records returned for a German field.
- Fixed USDA market prices and yield benchmarks.

These responses should not be used to drive farmer recommendations without a clear `demo`, `fallback` or `unavailable` status.

## 8. Data relationships and practical analytics

### Relationships already used

| Combined data | Insight |
|---|---|
| SMAP L4 + GPM IMERG | Current root-zone moisture relative to recent rainfall |
| Sentinel-2 NDVI + Sentinel-2 NDRE | Vegetation vigour and relative nitrogen-uptake signal |
| Sentinel-2 NDVI + yield zones | Persistent high and low productivity patterns |
| SoilGrids + GPM + nitrogen date | Leaching vulnerability and alerts |
| DEM + SoilGrids + heavy rain | Erosion screening |
| Open-Meteo + DWD | Short-term field decisions and official warning context |
| Sentinel-2 + ECOSTRESS | Fine-scale plant-water or evaporative-stress investigation |

### Useful future indicators

These are not all currently implemented:

- **Water stress confidence:** require agreement between SMAP percentile, NDMI and ECOSTRESS ESI before issuing a strong warning.
- **Nitrogen confidence:** issue an NDRE alert only when the NDMI and NDVI maps do not indicate water stress or bare soil.
- **Persistent weak zone:** flag an area only when it is low in at least two or three seasons.
- **Sowing readiness:** combine soil temperature, three-day rain, recent rain and field moisture.
- **Harvest readiness:** combine NDVI decline, GDD and crop record.
- **Field comparison:** rank fields by moisture status, vegetation trend, soil health and erosion risk.
- **Data confidence score:** show when a conclusion is based on one coarse cell, multiple satellite scenes or a complete time series.

## 9. Missing or limited capabilities

- Legal nitrogen ceiling and red-zone status are not available from the current Field Analytics data.
- Crop history for 2024 and 2025 is not consistently recorded for all fields.
- SMAP and GPM do not resolve variation inside a field.
- ECOSTRESS has irregular cloud-free overpasses and is still asynchronous through AppEEARS.
- Status precipitation and GDD use a fixed 2024 period rather than a user-selected or current season.
- The Data tab is a catalogue and exploration hub, not yet a trustworthy live-data dashboard for all 23 datasets.
- The app has a default deleted field ID in `App.tsx`, causing temporary 404 requests before the current field is resolved.
- The existing field analytics tests still trigger long real Sentinel-2 downloads and currently have two failures related to old report assumptions and a `unit` key.

## 10. Recommended farmer workflow

### Today

1. Open Field Map.
2. Read the four card values.
3. Check the top action cards.
4. Use one overlay at a time.
5. Open Field Health only when the field needs investigation.

### Season

1. Check the NDVI trend.
2. Compare the current moisture line with the usual range.
3. Inspect yield zones when planning soil samples or trials.
4. Check erosion and leaching before field operations.

### Inputs and compliance

1. Enter crop, yield target and Nmin.
2. Review the estimate and its breakdown.
3. Confirm the result against the official farm calculation.
4. Keep the Data tab for technical source inspection, not daily decision-making.

## 12. Audit conclusion

Field Analytics already supports meaningful field decisions when the farmer uses the Field Map and Field Health pages. Its strongest verified capabilities are:

- Real Sentinel-2 vegetation monitoring.
- Real SMAP L4 moisture comparison.
- Real GPM IMERG rainfall history.
- SoilGrids-based soil screening.
- DEM-based erosion screening.
- Open-Meteo forecast and DWD warning context.
- Derived yield-zone and risk indicators with visible limitations.

The Data tab should be treated as an experimental catalogue until its hardcoded and fallback responses are removed or clearly labelled. For the farmer-facing product, the condensed Map and Health workflow is more reliable and useful than exposing all 23 datasets as if they were equally operational.
